What Seedlist is

For investors

A short list of vetted projects, each with the same facts: sector, audience, B2B or B2C, scale, estimated growth, current traction and the amount being raised.

Filter, shortlist, take private notes, and open the founder’s live site without leaving the page.

For founders

No stage, no pitch tour. Submit your numbers and a Loom video. Only certified investors can open them.

You see how many investors viewed your project. You see who they are only when they choose to reach out.

Privacy

Projects and investors are hidden until you sign in. Competitors and the public can’t browse the list, and investors can’t see each other.

How it works

Four steps

  1. Apply as a founder or an investor.
  2. We verify your identity. Investors also need certification.
  3. Founders submit a project. Investors browse by sector.
  4. Investors express interest and the founder decides whether to connect.
